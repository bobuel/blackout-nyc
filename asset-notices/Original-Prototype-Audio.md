# Street-proof sound sources

The ten WAV files in `assets/audio` are original synthesized prototype sounds created by the editable `tools/art/build_audio.py` script: four footsteps, cloth handling, a door creak, contact, a shot, rail resonance and night air. No external recordings, sample libraries, hosted services or paid APIs were used.

The old root-level synthesized sounds remain editable prototype material. The current soundscape uses the revised `assets/audio/original` bank and `tools/art/build_soundscape.py`; see `Original-Soundscape.md`. Kenney's CC0 contact sounds still provide footsteps and punch contact. Field recordings for wind and fire are documented separately in this directory. Voice information remains written in the interface. Final audio and human experience gates remain open.
